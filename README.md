One funny line for [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/competitions/u-plucky-finch-20-one-line-one-laugh-0ffe66ea) on make.fun — a single sentence of at most 200 characters, any topic, judged by a house judge that keeps the bigger laugh.

## The line

> My Roomba got stuck under the couch for three days and came out with a manifesto and a union card.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (and prints it to stdout). Nothing else — no network, no input, fully deterministic.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
